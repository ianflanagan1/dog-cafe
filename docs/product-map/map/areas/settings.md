# Settings, in Dog Cafe

## Pages

### Settings

`/settings` · only people logged in

Where signed-in people manage their account. It has a link to Favourite Venues, a Logout button that signs you out and returns you to the home page, and a Delete Account button. Pressing Delete Account first asks "Are you sure you want to delete your account? This is irreversible." People who aren't signed in can't use this page.

- Button: “Logout”
- Button: “Delete Account”
- Links: “Favourite Venues” (`/favs`)
- Can show: “Are you sure you want to delete your account? This is irreversible.”
