# Other pages and things people do, in Dog Cafe

## Pages

### Error

No address of its own · anyone

Shown in place of a page when something goes wrong, for example when a page or venue can't be found, a form has expired, or a request isn't allowed. It shows only the error number, such as 404 or 403, under the site header.

### Suggest a feature

No address of its own · anyone

Shown after you submit a suggestion or bug report on the Suggest a feature page, thanking you with Thank you!

- Shows: “Thank you!”

## Things people do

### Opening the documentation

`/docs` · anyone

Going to the site's docs address takes you straight to the documentation, which is hosted on an external Notion page.

### Signing in with Google

`/redirect-google-login` · anyone

After you choose Sign in with Google on the Login page and approve it at Google, Google sends you back here. You're logged in and returned to the page you were on before. If the sign-in link has expired or was tampered with, you see an error page (403) instead.

### Signing in with Discord

`/redirect-discord-login` · anyone

After you choose Sign in with Discord on the Login page and approve it at Discord, Discord sends you back here. You're logged in and returned to the page you were on before. If the sign-in link has expired or was tampered with, you see an error page (403) instead.

### Logging out

`/logout` · only people logged in

Pressing Logout on the Account page logs you out and takes you to the home page. If the page had been open too long or the request wasn't genuine, you see an error page (403) instead.

### Deleting your account

`/account` · only people logged in

Pressing Delete Account on the Settings page and confirming the warning removes your account for good: your personal details and profile picture are erased, you're signed out, and you're taken back to the home page. Cancelling the warning leaves everything as it was. Only signed-in people can do this.
