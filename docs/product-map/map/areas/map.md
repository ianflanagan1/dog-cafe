# Map, in Dog Cafe

## Pages

### Map

`/map/<town>` · anyone

Dog-friendly places in a town on a map, reached from Map in the header or a town search. It starts centred on the town. Filters let you tick Cafes, Restaurants, Bars/Pubs and Open now, and Nearby moves the map to where you are. Selecting a venue opens a short summary with See details, which goes to its venue page.

- Shows: “Filters”, “Cafes”, “Restaurants”, “Bars/Pubs”, “Open now”, “Nearby”, “See details”
- Tick box: “Cafes”
- Tick box: “Restaurants”
- Tick box: “Bars/Pubs”
- Tick box: “Open now”
- Can show: “Searching...”
- Can show: “No results”
