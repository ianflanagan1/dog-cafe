# List, in Dog Cafe

## Pages

### List

`/<town>`, `/list/<town>` · anyone

Dog-friendly places in a town, as a list. You get here by searching for a town or choosing List in the header. The Filters let you tick Cafes, Restaurants, Bars/Pubs and Open now; the list updates as you change them, showing Searching... and then the venues, page by page, or No results.

- Shows: “Dog-friendly places in”, “Filters”, “Page”, “Cafes”, “Restaurants”, “Bars/Pubs”, “Open now”
- Tick box: “Cafes”
- Tick box: “Restaurants”
- Tick box: “Bars/Pubs”
- Tick box: “Open now”
- Can show: “Searching...”
- Can show: “No results”
